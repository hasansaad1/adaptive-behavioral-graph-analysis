PILOT HONEST SUMMARY
--------------------
This pilot shows the GAE learns to reconstruct benign behavioral graphs end-to-end on
57 graphs from 57 apps (46 train / 11 test split).
It is NOT a malware-detection result — the corpus contains no malware traces. The per-graph
reconstruction errors above are the benign baseline (future false-positive floor). Detection
evaluation requires malware traces and multi-session references per app.
